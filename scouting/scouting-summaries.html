    Trends, summaries & printouts

    Counts on their own don't tell you much. Three things turn them into a decision.

    Trends — is pressure rising?

    Tap Scouting in the sidebar, then Trends.

    1. Choose Trap counts or Sweeps.
    2. Pick the pest — Black-Headed Fireworm, Sparganothis Fruitworm, Spotted Fireworm, and so on.

    You get a flight graph — a weekly average per trap — above four figures:

    Figure Meaning
    Total caught Everything counted this season
    Avg per trap The average across traps
    Weeks recorded How many weeks have data — 3 of 4 scouted
    Trend Rising or falling

    Show as a table swaps the graph for the numbers.

    A zero and a gap mean different things

    Blossom is explicit about this: each bar is that week's count averaged over every trap read for that pest. A recorded 0 is a result and is drawn as zero. A dash is a week with no reading — nobody looked, which is not the same as finding nothing.

    'Weeks recorded: 3 of 4 scouted' is a data-quality warning

    It's telling you the trend is built from three weeks out of four visits. A rising trend on patchy data deserves another look before you spray on it.

    Summaries

    Inside a report, write up what the counts mean:

    • Plant Summary
    • Insect Summary
    • Weeds Summary
    • Disease Summary
    • Marsh Comparison — how beds or marshes compare this round

    These are what appear in the Reports tab's summary columns, and what a grower actually reads later.

    Printing a report

    Open a report and tap Print at the top for a clean copy to print or save — useful for the crew, or to send to a handler.
