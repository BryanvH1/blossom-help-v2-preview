    Applications

    Everything you put on a bed is recorded here — pesticides and nutrients together. Tap Applications in the sidebar.

    This is the biggest change from the old Blossom

    Sprays and fertilizer used to live in two different places: Pest Management for one, Nutrients for the other. In V2 they share one screen, and you filter to the kind you want.

    Finding your way around

    The screen has two tabs:

    • Applications — the ledger of what's been applied.
    • PUR Transfer — sending pesticide use to Ocean Spray or Agrian.

    On the Applications tab, two rows of filters narrow what you see:

    Filter Options
    Kind All · Pesticide · Nutrients
    Status All · Requested · Approved · Completed

    The status chips on the cards themselves read a little differently — Open, Awaiting approval, Completed — so don't expect the two lists to match word for word.

    There's also a search box — search by product, target pest, bed or applicator — which is usually faster than filtering when you know what you're looking for.

    What a ledger entry shows

    Each application in the list carries the date, the applicator, a kind chip (PESTICIDE or NUTRIENT), the products, and — for a spray — when its REI clears:

    Weedar 64 · Provixo 480 SC · REI clears Wed, Sep 30 · Rate 7.82 fl oz/ac · Coverage 21.31 ac · Target Fireworm · Completed

    A nutrient entry shows the same skeleton with a bed count and the analysis instead of a target: 5-10-25 · 7 Beds · Rate 294 lbs/ac · Coverage 25.82 ac · N / P₂O₅ / K₂O.

    REI clearance is on the ledger itself

    You don't have to open an application, or dig out the posting sheet, to find out when a bed comes back into play. The ledger says it on every row — and on the nearest ones it says it in plain words: REI clears today, REI clears tomorrow. A product with no interval on file reads REI unknown, which means go and read the label, not that there is no restriction.

    Work orders sit here too

    Not every entry is something that has already happened. Work orders — jobs written down in advance — appear in the same list, dated by when they are due rather than when they were done:

    Work order #1037 · Target September 11, 2026 · Products applied: Drexel Carbaryl 4L · Badge SC · … · 1 application · 18.47 ac · Open

    They are worth understanding separately, because a work order is where Blossom works out the combined re-entry and harvest restrictions for a tank mix.

    Work orders

    Recording something

    Tap New application and you get four choices, not two — because you can either record what happened or order what should:

    Log (it happened) Work order (it should happen)
    Pesticide Log pesticide New pesticide work order
    Nutrient Log nutrient application New nutrient work order

    The two work order options are how a planned job gets onto the ledger — see Work orders.

    The two Log options are below:

    • Pesticide application

      Products, adjuvants, method, beds, applicator, target pests and weather.

      Open

    • Nutrient application

      Fertilizer, rate, beds — and whether it counts toward nutrient management.

      Open
