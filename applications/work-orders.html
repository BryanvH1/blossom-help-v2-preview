    Work orders

    A work order is a spray job written down before it happens: the mix, the beds, who is applying it and when. It sits in the Applications ledger alongside finished applications, and it turns into one once the work is recorded.

    Writing one

    Tap Applications → New application, then either New pesticide work order or New nutrient work order. You fill it in the same way you'd log an application that had happened — beds, products, rates — except you give it a target date instead of a date applied, and nobody has done the work yet.

    Spotting one in the ledger

    Work-order entries look different from finished applications. Instead of a rate and a target, they show:

    Work order #1037 · Target September 11, 2026 · Applicator: Owen Keane Products applied: Drexel Carbaryl 4L · Badge SC · Sil-MATRIX LC · TIDE PPZ+Azoxy · 1 application · 18.47 ac · Open

    The date is labelled Target rather than being the date it happened — the job is still ahead of you. If nobody has been assigned yet, the card says No applicator assigned instead of a name.

    Two vocabularies for status

    The status filter at the top of the ledger offers Requested · Approved · Completed, but the chips on work-order cards read Open, Awaiting approval and Completed. They describe the same journey in slightly different words, so don't be thrown when a card says Open and the filter doesn't.

    Reading a work order

    Tap the card to open it.

    The mix

    Each product in the tank is listed with everything the applicator needs:

    Shown Means
    Group FRAC 11, FRAC 3 The resistance group — for rotating chemistry
    6.45 fl oz/ac The rate
    32.44 fl oz for 5.03 ac The total to mix, already worked out
    REI 12 h Restricted-entry interval
    PHI 45 d Pre-harvest interval

    The beds

    Under ON 4 BEDS every bed on the order is listed with its acreage, its variety and the state of that line — Planned until it's recorded.

    A line at the bottom tells you how many of them can be drawn on the Map: "2 of 4 beds on this order have a shape — all 4 are in the beds panel." Beds without a boundary still appear on the order and still get sprayed; they just can't be shaded on the map.

    Restrictions for this mix

    This is the part worth reading twice. When several products go on together, Blossom works out the restriction that covers the whole treated area and states it plainly:

    Binding hold: REI 48 h — Badge SC · do not enter without PPE until Sun, Sep 13, 2026

    Earliest harvest: Mon, Oct 26, 2026 — longest PHI is 45 d (TIDE PPZ+Azoxy).

    Two rules are doing the work, and How this is worked out spells them out:

    • The longest REI governs a co-application (40 CFR Part 170). One re-entry clock covers everything once the products go on together — you cannot treat the 4-hour product as 4 hours because it shared a tank with a 48-hour one.
    • The longest PHI sets the earliest harvest, and the REI is used instead if it happens to run longer.

    The label governs, always

    A label may set its own restriction for a specific tank-mix combination, and that provision wins for that combination. Blossom's figure is a calculation from the products you entered — it is a check on your reading of the labels, never a replacement for it.

    Recording the work

    When the job is done, tap Record completion.

    1. Date Completed — required.
    2. Completed By — pick the applicator. The list shows certification numbers, so Owen Keane (MA-14542) rather than just a name.
    3. # Injections, Temp (°F) and Wind (MPH) if you're recording them.
    4. Weather Conditions — tick what applied: Sunny · P Cloudy · Clear · Cloudy · OVC · Foggy · Calm · Rain · Lt Breeze · Drizzle.
    5. Under Beds to mark complete, tick each bed that was actually applied on this date. Each one shows its product and acreage.
    6. Tap Complete Work Order.

    You can finish an order in stages

    The bed list is a set of tick boxes precisely because a spray often runs over more than one day. Tick only the beds you did, save, and come back for the rest — the order tracks it as Lines: 2 of 4 done until everything is in. A Target date that has passed is flagged overdue, so a half-finished order won't quietly disappear.
